<product_requirements>
The Doord application is a marketplace connecting homeowners with home service providers, aiming to streamline service browsing, quote requests, and order management for homeowners, and lead/customer/quotation management for providers.

**Homeowner Portal:** Features include a landing page, service browsing with 25+ categories, provider profiles (ratings, images, pricing), a quotation-to-order workflow, dedicated authentication, a dashboard (Home, Orders, Book service, Messages, Reports & Issues, Settings), a visual How It Works section, an enhanced trust-focused hero section, and a dynamic reviews section.

**Service Provider CRM:** Includes a landing page, a dashboard with sales, calendar, customers, invoicing, and analytics, lead and quotation management, separate authentication, a visual How It Works for business growth, and a sales-focused dashboard preview.

**Overall Design:** The application uses a white and blue theme, aiming for a high-end UI/UX with abstract visuals, micro-animations, depth, bold typography, ample whitespace, and full responsiveness across desktop and mobile.
</product_requirements>
